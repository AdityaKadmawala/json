# Registrations a Philippine dialysis center needs, and whether each can be looked up publicly

Tested from this research environment on **2026-11-14**. "Tested" means I sent a few rate-limited requests (at least 1 s apart) and nothing more. I did not try to get around Cloudflare or bot protection, CAPTCHAs or logins. "403 (Cloudflare)" means the site's bot wall refused the request.

**Key takeaways**
* The only registries that are **public, machine-readable and reachable here** are: the PhilHealth accredited-FDC list, the CDA cooperative masterlist, the FDA verification search, PSE Edge (for listed parents), and GLEIF LEI records, which carry SEC registration numbers for a few multinationals (FMC Philippines `A200108421`; B. Braun Medical Supplies `0000130178`).
* The **DOH LTO** list, which is where machine/station counts per center live, and **SEC** records, which hold directors, shareholders and AFS revenue, are behind Cloudflare. An FOI request or paid SEC copies are the realistic routes.
