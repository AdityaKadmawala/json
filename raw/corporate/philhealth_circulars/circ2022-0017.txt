 SUBJECT                Phi!Health Coverage for Outpatient Hemodialysis Benefit to a

 I.   RATIONALE

       Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (CKD 5) is a fatal condition affecting millions of people
       worldwide. It is the seventh leading cause of death among Filipinos. According to the
       National Kidney and Transplant Institute (NKTI), one Filipino develops chronic renal
       failure every hour or about 120 Filipinos per million population every year. Latest
       estimates show that around 2.3 million Filipinos have chronic kidney disease (CKD). In
       2016, more than 36,000 patients were on dialysis treatment which reflects a 15% increase
      .in the number of patients in just one year.

      CKD 5 can be treated with kidney transplantation or renal dialysis. While kidney
      transplant is a definitive treatment, the low levels of organ donation, lack of infrastructure,
      and high cost of transplantation are baniers to access. Hence, renal dialysis remains the
      most viable option for renal patients in the Philippines.

      The COVID-19 pandemic continues to challenge the health system in our country and
      has led to devastating impacts on poverty and loss of human life. The cost of
      hemodialysis (HD) can be a financial banier in accessing healthcare and cari push the
      patients and their families to financial hardship.

      Pursuant to the Universal Health Care (UHQ Act (Republic Act No. 11223), Phi!Health
      is mandated to ensure financial risk protection for all Filipinos. This has been further
      emphasized by Proclamation No. 1218, s. 2020 "Further Extending the period of a State
      of Calamity throughout the Philippines due to Corona Virus Disease 2019 declared under
      Proclamation No. 1021, s. 2020" effective until12 September 2022.

      Cognizant to the needs of its members, Phi!Health intends to increase the coverage of
      hemodialysis while balancing the available resources to fund this benefit The Phi!Health
      Board of Directors, through Phi!Health Board Resolution No. 2718, s. 2022, approved
      the payment of·benefit claims for hemodialysis services up to a maximum of 144 sessions
      forCY2022.

       II.    OBJECTIVES

              This Phi!Health Grcular aims to establish the guidelines on availment of Phi!Health
              benefit for Outpatient Hemodialysis Benefit Package to a maximum of 144 sessions for
              calendar year (CY) 2022.

       III.   SCOPE

              This policy applies to Phi!Health members and their dependents with chronic kidney
              disease stage 5 (CKD5) who are currently enrolled in the Phi!Health Dialysis Database
              (PDD). This shall cover members and their dependents as defined in Sections 6.1 and
              9.1 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of the UHC Act to provide that every
              Filipino "shall be granted immediate eligibility". This policy covers for all hemodialysis
              beyond the ninety (90) to a maximum of one-hundred-fatty-four (144) sessions for CY
              2022.

       IV.    DEFINITION OF TERMS

              A     Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (CKD 5)- is an end stage renal disease (ESRD)
                    with a glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 15 mV minute or less. At this advanced
                    stage of kidney disease, the kidneys have lost nearly all their ability to do their job
                    effectively, and eventually dialysis or a kidney transplant is needed to live. This kind
                    of kidney failure is permanent and is usually caused by diabetes or high blood
                    pressure (Phi!Health Grcular No. 2016-007).

              B.    Phi!Health Dialysis Database (PDD) - is a system that collects data on members
                    and dependents diagnosed of CKD 5 (previously !mown as ESRD) who are
                    prescribed with hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, or other renal replacement
                    therapy (Phi!Health Grcular No. 2016-007).

       V.     POLICY STATEMENTS

              A    Patients with CKD 5 shall be allowed to avail of the maximum of one hundred
                   fatty-four (144) hemodialysis sessions for CY 2022 based on the prescription of
                   their attending physician.

              B.   Patients with CKD5 shall be registered in the PDD in accordance with Phi!Health
                   Grcular No. 2016- 0007.

              G    The 91st to 144th additional sessions shall be exclusively used for outpatient
                   hemo.dialysis.

              D.   Any unused sessions will not be carried over to CY 2023.

              E.   Prior to publication of this Phi!Health Grcular, qualified CKD 5 patients who
                   already used their 91st to 144th HD sessions and whose HD benefits have not yet
                   been deducted from their medical bill, may directly file claims with Phi!Health
                   Regional/Branch Office or Local Health Insurance Office (IBIO) based on
                   existing guidelines for directly filed claims (Phi!Health Grcular No. 20 s. 2014 on
                   ACRPolicy No.4- Directly Filed Cairns for All Case Rates and Return to Sender).
