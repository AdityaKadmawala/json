Comprehensive Care at

ZERO COST

Hemodialysis package at CuraKidney includes the following with no additional cost:

EPO

EPO injections administered with each session effectively correct anemia, boost hemoglobin levels, and significantly reduce the need for blood transfusions.

IV Sucrose

Intravenous iron sucrose (IV Sucrose) is given during each session to replenish iron stores, refill your iron levels, improve how your EPO medication works, and bypass the issues that oral iron can cause.

Monthly Lab Tests

Free laboratory tests for regular screening include CBC, Pre and Post Dialysis BUN, Serum Creatinine, Serum Sodium, Electrolytes, Serum Albumin, Hepatitis Profile, Serum Iron/Ferritin/TIBC.

HCV & HBV Screening

Free regular screenings for HCV (hepatitis C) and HBV (hepatitis B) support dialysis treatment and protect patient health.

Personalized Diet Plan

Personalized diet plan is provided by an expert renal dietitian, designed to accelerate your recovery and enhance your well-being.

Discounted Car Ride

All CuraKidney patients receive an exclusive discount through our GrabCar partnership, making travel to and from dialysis easier, safer, and more affordable.

The Cura Standard
of Water

Water is one of the most important parts of every dialysis treatment. During hemodialysis, patients are exposed to 300-600 liters of water weekly.

That’s why CuraKidney uses Double Pass Reverse Osmosis, an advanced purification system that delivers ultra-pure water by filtering it through two stages.

✔ Double-stage purification
Water passes through reverse osmosis twice for an extra level of purification.

✔ Ultrapure Water Standard
Meets ultrapure dialysis standards—up to 1,000× stricter for bacteria and 16× stricter for endotoxins.

✔ Designed for safer dialysis
Because cleaner water means safer dialysis and greater peace of mind.

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much do I need to pay for my dialysis?

With PhilHealth’s extended dialysis coverage, you pay nothing, PHP 0 — ensuring essential, high-quality dialysis care is accessible and free for every Filipino.

Where are you located and how do I get there?

You can find us conveniently located at the Ground Floor (G/F) of Ayala Malls Feliz, Pasig City. Near the Taxi Bay and below Starbucks.

Ayala Malls Feliz is easily accessible by various modes of transport, including public transportation and private vehicles. There is ample parking available at the mall. We also have a branch at Camaro Square, 87 Camaro St, Novaliches, Quezon City. We look forward to serving you at CuraKidney.

What documents do I need to start my dialysis at CuraKidney?

  • Nephrologist Referral and Prescription
  • Medical Records: Recent laboratory results (within one month), including a Hepatitis Profile (within 6 months) are required.
  • Dialysis Data Sheet from your previous dialysis center for the last three sessions
  • List of all medications
  • Valid ID and PhilHealth Number

Are Single-use Dialyzers used for all dialysis sessions?

Yes. Every dialysis treatment at CuraKidney begins with a brand-new, single-use dialyzer that is opened, used only for your session, and then properly disposed of — never reused — to minimize contamination risk and ensure consistent, safe treatment every time.

Is CuraKidney PhilHealth-accredited?

Yes, CuraKidney is PhilHealth-accredited. With PhilHealth’s extended dialysis coverage, qualified patients can avail of dialysis sessions at PHP 0, making essential, high-quality dialysis care accessible for every Filipino.
