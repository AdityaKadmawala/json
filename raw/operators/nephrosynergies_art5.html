Visit our website : GLOBAL NEPHRO TRAINING CENTER (GNTC)


For a Challenging and Rewarding Career in Nursing
The GNTC dialysis nurse training program is a four (4) month theoretical and hands-on training on the various aspects of hemodialysis. It includes Initiation, Monitoring, Termination, and Care of the dialysis patient.

Nurses who finish the duration of the training course are deemed equipped with the proper knowledge, experience and skills to carry out hemodialysis orders and care of the patient for the entire dialysis process.

In Global Nephro Training Center the nurse trainees will take ownership of care programs for dialysis patients with ESRD. They will also work closely with dieticians, nephrologists, machine technicians and re use technicians.


DIALYSIS NURSE TRAINING COURSE
  • Module 1: Theory and Simulation (18 days)

On-the-Job Training ( OJT )
  • Module 2: Monitoring, Observation and Priming (15 days)
  • Module 3: Cannulation and Priming (30days)
  • Module 4: Skills Mastery (30 days)
