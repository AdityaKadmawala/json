Fresenius Kidney Care Dialysis Services

We delivered on our commitments for 2023

Visit the Fresenius Kidney Care Dialysis Center

Fresenius Kidney Care offers dialysis treatment and support for individuals living with chronic kidney disease and ESRD. We have a network of clinics globally and operates 24 Dialysis centers locally in the Philippines. As a division of Fresenius Medical Care Asia Pacific, Fresenius Kidney Care is part of a network of companies dedicated to providing superior care that improves the quality of life for every patient, every day. With access to the vast resources of our global parent company, Fresenius Medical Care AG & Co. KGaA, the world’s leading innovator in kidney care and the largest provider of dialysis products and services, we’re uniquely equipped to provide comprehensive quality care to all of our patients.
